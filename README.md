# Privacy Policy for OrgaNice

## Data We Handle

OrgaNice is designed to work locally on your browser. To provide its features, the extension needs to access and store certain data:

* **Collections, Tabs, and Notes:** The extension saves the URLs, titles of your tabs, and any notes you write. This is the core function of the extension.
* **Browser History:** The extension's search feature queries your browser history locally to provide comprehensive search results.

## How Data is Stored

All data you create (collections, notes) and the data we access (browser history) **stays on your local machine or in your browser's sync storage** (`chrome.storage.sync`).

This data is synced across your devices only if you are logged into your browser profile (e.g., Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge profile). This syncing mechanism is provided by the browser itself, not by us.

## Data Sharing

We **do not** collect, store, sell, or transmit any of your personal data to any third-party servers. All data remains within your control, stored locally or within your browser's private sync area.
